RECIPE of American Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 200 g butter (softened)
  • 150 g brown sugar
  • 100 g white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300 g flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 200 g chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the softened butter with brown sugar and white sugar until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla extract, and mix well.
  4. In another bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture and mix until combined.
  6. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  7. Form small balls of dough and place them on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, leaving space between each cookie.
  8. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are golden but the center is still soft.
  9. Let them cool for a few minutes before eating.

Tips

If you want them softer, don't overbake them – they continue to cook while cooling.

If everything turned out well, your cookies are ready. Enjoy!
